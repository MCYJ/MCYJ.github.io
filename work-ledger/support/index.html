Work Ledger support

Start a record, review today’s time, or contact RushLabs.

Start, switch, and stop

Enter a work name and tap Start. When you move to another project, enter its name and tap Switch work. The previous entry ends when the next one starts. Tap Stop when you finish.

Review today’s records

A gap means there is no entry for that time. Open a completed record to correct its times, or use Add missing time to add an entry. If times overlap or the device clock changes, check the times before relying on the record.

Share today’s CSV

When today has completed records, choose Share today’s CSV and select a destination. The file includes the part of each completed record that falls within today and preserves its original times in separate columns.

Get help

Include your app version, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Keep client names and work details out of your message and screenshots.
