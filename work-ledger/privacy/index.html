Privacy policy

Work Ledger by RushLabs

Information on your device

Work Ledger keeps the work names you enter, start and end times, recorded time zone information, any active record, and corrections you make, including their reasons. The app uses this data to maintain your personal records. It needs no account and does not send your ledger to RushLabs. The app does not use ads, analytics, location tracking, or a RushLabs cloud sync service.

Copies and sharing

If you share a CSV, you choose the destination in the system share sheet. The receiving service or person handles its own copy. Depending on your device settings, system backups may also include app data.

Remove your data

To remove the app and its data from the device, choose Delete App in iOS. Offload App keeps app data. Delete any CSV files or system backups separately wherever you saved them.

Support and privacy requests

If you email RushLabs, we use your email address and the information you send to reply and handle your request. You can use the same address to request deletion of information you sent us. Please leave out client names and work records unless essential.

Apple processes the optional purchase for CSV export. The app checks purchase and restore status through StoreKit.
