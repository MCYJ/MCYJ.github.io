Tactics Canvas support

Choose a court and edit the play

Choose Basketball, Soccer, or Grid. Drag a player to move it. Choose Draw a route and drag on the board to create an arrow. Tap an arrow or select a route from the list to edit or delete it.

Work across stages

Duplicate a stage to try a new setup while keeping the previous one. Use Undo or Redo to step through changes. The board saves on your device.

Export a play

Open Share play and choose the current stage as a PNG or all stages as a PDF. Use your device’s share sheet to save or send the file.

Report a problem

Include the device model, system version, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. Leave out player names and other personal details.
