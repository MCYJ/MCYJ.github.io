Tactics Canvas privacy policy

Your board and app data

Tactics Canvas stores your formations, routes, stages, and editing history on your device. RushLabs does not receive your board through the app. Tactics Canvas does not use accounts or advertising. Your system may back up app data based on your device settings.

Files you export

Tactics Canvas creates PNG and PDF exports on your device. If you share an export, the app or service you choose handles that copy according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Support email

If you contact RushLabs, we receive your email address and the details you choose to include to respond to your request. Keep personal information out of support emails.
