Comic Spread Reader privacy information

Imported comics and reading progress

The reader is designed to process CBZ files on your device. Page order, reading direction, and progress are intended to stay in the app’s local storage.

Files you choose to share

If you share or export a copy through your device, the app or service you choose handles that copy. Your device may also back up local app data, depending on your system settings.

Support messages

If you email RushLabs, we receive your contact address and the details you include so we can respond. Please leave comic files and page images out of your message.
