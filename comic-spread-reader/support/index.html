Support for Comic Spread Reader

Opening a comic

A CBZ file is a ZIP archive of images. Keep your original file and use a copy when troubleshooting a damaged archive. The reader is being developed for CBZ files you already have access to.

When a spread reads in the wrong order

Check the comic’s original reading direction. A cover is typically read on its own, even when the following pages are arranged side by side.

Reporting a problem

Include your device model, operating system version, and the steps you took. Describe what happened without attaching the comic, its pages, or any personal information.
