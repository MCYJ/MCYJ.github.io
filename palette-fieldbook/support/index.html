Palette Fieldbook · Support

Palette Fieldbook keeps a local notebook of colours sampled from your own photos. Choose a photo from Files, inspect its swatches and record the purpose of a colour.

The working image is converted to sRGB and may be reduced in size. Fully transparent pixels are excluded; partially transparent pixels are composited over white. HEX and RGB describe these working-image values, not calibrated paint, print or physical colours.

The contrast comparison is a numerical sRGB calculation. It does not diagnose colour vision or certify an entire design as accessible.

For help, include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Describe the file format and size without sending private photos, backups or documents.
