Palette Fieldbook · Privacy

Palette Fieldbook processes selected photos and colour notebooks locally on your device. RushLabs does not operate an account system or upload endpoint for the app. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

The app keeps an app-owned copy of selected photos, sampled colours, points and notes for local use. Selecting a photo does not alter the original outside the app.

If you choose to export or share, iOS passes the output to the destination you select. That destination and any device backup or cloud synchronization you enabled are governed by their own settings and policies.

Remove local notebooks in the app or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Copies already exported or included in device backups must be managed separately at those destinations.

If you email support, RushLabs receives the information you choose to include and uses it to respond. Do not send sensitive photos or identification documents. Contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence.
