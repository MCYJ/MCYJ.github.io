QR Label Press privacy information

Data you enter

QR generation and PDF rendering run on the device. A Wi-Fi or contact label contains the information you choose to encode.

Files you export

When you save or share a PDF, the destination app or service you select handles that copy. Remove copies you no longer need from those locations.

Support requests

If you email support, RushLabs receives your email address and the information you choose to include so it can respond. Please leave Wi-Fi passwords and other private QR data out of support messages.
