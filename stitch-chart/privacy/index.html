Stitch Chart Studio privacy policy

Charts on your device

Stitch Chart Studio stores the chart and its editing history on your device. The app works without an account or advertising. Your device may back up app data according to its system settings.

Files you share

The app creates PNG and PDF exports on your device. When you share a copy, the app or service you choose handles that file under its own privacy policy.

Support email

RushLabs receives the email address and details you choose to send when you contact support. We use those details to respond to your request.
