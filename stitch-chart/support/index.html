Stitch Chart Studio support

Draw and revise a chart

Choose a thread color and drag across the 64 × 64 grid. Select Eraser to clear cells. Use Undo and Redo to revisit a change.

Move around the grid

Turn on Pan and zoom to move around the chart. Zoom in on individual cells when you need to work in more detail.

Save or print a copy

Choose Share chart to send the complete chart as a PNG or to open a printable PDF. The PDF includes four labeled sheets and a thread key. Use the coordinates to place the sheets in the correct order.

Report a problem

Send the device model, operating system and app versions, and the steps that caused the problem. Keep personal information out of your email.
