Number Craft Privacy

Number Craft runs practice on your device without an account. Answers are used to check the current problem and are cleared when you move to the next one. The app stores whether you finished or skipped the guide in local settings.

RushLabs does not receive your practice data. The app has no ads or analytics. Delete the app to remove its local settings. If you email support, we use what you send to respond to your request.
