Quilt Layout Studio privacy policy

Files on your device

The app stores your layout and copies of imported swatches in its own storage. Your device may back up this data according to your system settings.

Accounts and advertising

The app works without an account, advertising or analytics. RushLabs does not receive your quilt layouts or swatches from the app.

Support email

If you contact support by email, RushLabs uses the details you send to respond to your request.
