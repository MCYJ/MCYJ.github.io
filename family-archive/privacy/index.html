Privacy policy

Your family records are stored in the app’s database on your device. Family Archive Studio has no account, advertising, or analytics. RushLabs does not receive your archive through the app. When you share a backup, the destination you choose receives that file and its contents. If you email support, RushLabs uses the information you send to respond.
