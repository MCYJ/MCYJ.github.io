Garden Grid privacy

Garden Grid stores bed dimensions, planting positions, dates, season plans and onboarding preferences in local storage. The app has no user account, advertising, or analytics. It does not send your plans to RushLabs. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your operating system can back up app data under your device settings.
