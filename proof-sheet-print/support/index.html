ProofSheet Print · Support

Choose photos from Files, then select the paper size and grid. Check each photo’s fit or crop and the number or filename label before creating the PDF.

Open the PDF preview to inspect the pages before sharing or printing. A preview is not proof that a physical print has the correct dimensions.

For a size check, print at Actual size / 100%, disable Fit to page, and measure the printed 100 mm line with a ruler. Printer and paper settings may change the scale.

For help, include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Describe the file type and size without sending personal content.
