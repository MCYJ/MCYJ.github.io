CUE SCROLL

Privacy

On-device script data

Cue Scroll processes scripts on your device. RushLabs does not receive your scripts or operate a script-upload or account service for this app. The app contains no advertising or analytics service and does not automatically translate your text. It does not collect camera or microphone recordings.

Local records

App-owned storage contains imported text and original-file copies, names, edited text, cues, reading positions, display and scroll settings, editing drafts, undo history and guide status. Imported external files remain unchanged. Removing one script can leave a copy in undo history; the all-local-data deletion action also clears undo and app-owned copies.

Exports and backups

When you request export or backup, the app creates a local TXT or JSON file and opens system sharing. A JSON backup includes the original copies and text retained by drafts and undo; it is not an encrypted archive. You choose whether and where to send it. RushLabs does not receive these exported files unless you choose to send one to us.

System services and destinations

A Files provider may download a file from a cloud location you select. The sharing destination, device backup and any cloud settings use their own policies. On-device processing does not mean a copy you choose to export can never leave the device. This app does not automatically synchronise your script library.

Restore and delete

Restore checks the backup and asks for confirmation before replacing the current local records, including drafts and undo. It does not merge libraries. Deleting app-owned data or removing the app does not delete external originals, shared copies or copies held in a device backup; manage those at their destination.

Support and this website

If you email RushLabs, the information you choose to include is used to answer the request. Avoid sending private scripts, backups, passwords or identity documents. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email. This static website has no analytics, advertising, forms, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process connection information under its own policies.
