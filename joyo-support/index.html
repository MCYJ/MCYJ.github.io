Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026. This policy covers Joyo Kanji 2136 by RushLabs. Some features described here depend on your installed version.

Learning and device storage

Your bookmarks, mistakes, settings, session progress and learning history are stored on your device. Learning does not require a RushLabs account. The app schedules optional study reminders locally. Audio uses the speech engine and Japanese voices available on your device. Your operating system controls backups, notifications and installed voices.

Purchases

Apple and Google process purchases under their own privacy policies. Store transaction and entitlement information lets the app recognize purchases and restore eligible access. Earlier versions offer subscriptions. You can manage renewals in your store account. The Android update offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Payment card details are handled by the store.

Advertising in the Android update

Ad-supported Android versions use Google AdMob to show a banner. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic or performance information and device identifiers. Google uses these data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts data in transit. When consent options are required, the app presents Google’s privacy choices. You can reopen available advertising privacy options in Settings. When ad removal is active, the app skips banner requests. The iOS update does not request banners.

Storage and deletion

To clear learning progress use the reset option in Settings. To remove locally stored app data, delete the app or clear its storage in your device settings. Store purchase history remains under the control of Apple or Google. Contact support@loverush.kr for privacy questions.
