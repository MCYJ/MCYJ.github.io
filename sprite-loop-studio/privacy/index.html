Privacy policy

Sprite Loop Studio saves your artwork on your device. The app has no account, advertising, or analytics SDK and does not send artwork or personal data to RushLabs. If you share a PNG, the destination you choose handles that file under its own terms. If you email support, RushLabs uses the information you send to respond.
