Field Record · Privacy policy

The app stores your project details, observations, issue status, and photos you choose on this device.

The app does not send your records to RushLabs. System backups may include app data, depending on your device settings. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Delete any copies you kept or shared at their destinations.

If you contact us by email, we use the information you send to respond and handle your request. You can use the same address to ask us to delete information you sent.
