Hear the phrase. Then try it.

Phrase Loop is in development for iPhone and iPad. It is designed to repeat a short A–B section of a WAV file from your device so you can listen and rehearse at your own pace.

Listen back to your take

The planned 0.8× speed option slows the reference while aiming to preserve pitch. With microphone permission, you can record a take and switch between it and the reference to compare by ear.
