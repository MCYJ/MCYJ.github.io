Phrase Loop privacy information

Your audio files

Phrase Loop is designed to process the WAV files you choose on your device. Keep a separate copy of files that you want to retain outside the app.

Microphone and recorded takes

Microphone access is intended for recording a take when you choose to record. The app is being developed to keep those recordings on the device for comparison playback.

Support email

When you contact RushLabs, we receive your email address and the details you send so we can reply. Describe the issue without attaching recordings or other personal information.
