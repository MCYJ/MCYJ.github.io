Measure Notes · Privacy policy

The selected photo and the dimension values, units, and positions you enter are stored on your device. The app does not send them to RushLabs.

System backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local data. Delete any exported or shared copies at their destinations.

If you email us, RushLabs uses the information you send to respond and handle your request. You can use the same email address to ask us to delete information you sent.
