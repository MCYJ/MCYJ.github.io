Privacy policy

Home Rhythm: Cleaning Plan by RushLabs

What the app stores

Home Rhythm stores room and task names you enter, repeat intervals, estimated durations, session state, and completion history on your device to run the planner. RushLabs does not collect this content through the app. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

Files you choose to share

If you share a backup, the file contains your planner data. The app hands it to the system share sheet, and you choose where it goes. If you restore a backup, the app reads the file you select. The file service or recipient you choose handles its own copy. Device backups may also include app data, depending on your system settings.

Retention and deletion

Home Rhythm keeps its working data in app storage. To remove the data from this device, delete the app. Offloading an app on iOS retains its data. To remove exported files or device backups, delete them separately through the service or device settings where they are kept.

Support and privacy requests

If you email RushLabs, we use the information you send to respond and resolve your request. You can use the same address to request deletion of information you sent us. Please leave out your planner contents unless they are essential to your request.
