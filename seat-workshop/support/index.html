Seat Workshop support

Start a plan

Explore the sample plan or choose Start an empty plan from the menu. Add your tables and guests, then select a guest and tap an open seat.

Move or swap a guest

Tap the guest’s current seat and choose Select to move or swap. Tap an open seat to move them, or choose Swap seats on another occupied seat.

Import or share a CSV

Save guest names in a UTF-8 CSV with a name column. Choose Import guests from CSV to add them. Use Share seating CSV to save or share the current plan.
