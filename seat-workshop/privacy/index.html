Seat Workshop privacy policy

Seating plans stay on the device

The app saves names, tables and seating assignments in its own storage. Your system settings may allow device backups.

Import and share

The app reads a CSV you select. When you share a seating report, the app or service you choose can access that copy.

Support email

RushLabs receives the message and contact details you send by email and uses them to reply.
