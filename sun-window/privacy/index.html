Sun Window privacy

Sun Window calculates solar schedules on your device. Saved locations and onboarding preferences stay in local app storage. The app does not request location permission or use accounts, advertising, or analytics. Deleting the app removes data held by the app on the device. The operating system may back up data according to your settings. For privacy questions, contact june1012june@gmail.com
