Pitch Desk privacy

Pitch Desk reads the microphone while listening to estimate pitch. Audio samples are processed in memory on your device. The app does not save or transmit recordings. Your A4 reference, tuning target, and onboarding preference are stored on your device. You can remove those preferences by deleting the app. Pitch Desk has no account, advertising, or analytics. For privacy questions, contact june1012june@gmail.com
