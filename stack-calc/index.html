RPN · SCIENCE · FIXED WORD

A stack you can inspect.

Keep the input, the values and the steps in view. Stack Calc combines RPN calculation, scientific functions and fixed-width bit tools in one local workbench.

Illustrative RPN example

X5
2 ENTER 3 + → X = 5

Enter the first value, enter the second, then choose the operation.

01

Know what a value means

Exact integers and approximate real values are labelled separately. Inspect 8-, 16-, 32- or 64-bit patterns, choose signed or unsigned interpretation, and keep the radix and angle setting visible.

02

Read the path to the result

Browse up to 500 successful commands and inspect a selected step without changing the working stack. Applying a step is a separate action. Up to 100 calculation undo steps remain available; a successful new command can replace the redo branch.

03

Carry the work, not an account

Keep calculations and presets on your device. Export a plain JSON backup through the system share sheet. There is no RushLabs account, advertising, subscription or analytics service in the app.
