STACK CALC

Privacy

01

Who operates the app

RushLabs develops Stack Calc. Contact june1012june@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. This policy describes the local-data design of the version being prepared, not a claim that an App Store release is already available.

02

What stays on the device

The app stores entries, exact/approximate values, calculation commands, mode/word/angle settings, tape and undo state, preset names/commands/history, language preferences and guide status in app-owned local storage. RushLabs does not operate an account, calculation-upload, advertising or analytics service for this app. User-entered material is not automatically translated or sent to a translation service.

03

Files you choose

Restore reads a backup selected through Files and validates it before replacing the current book. The external original is not edited. Recovery may retain an app-owned damaged-original safety copy; temporary transaction files can remain if cleanup fails. Intentional retained copies can be removed explicitly and are not deleted by an ordinary cleanup retry.

04

Plain backups and sharing

A JSON backup is readable plain text, not an encrypted vault. It can include private entries and historical values, commands and presets, including retained undo information. The app keeps a small completed-export cache, normally the current and previous set, and preserves a file while sharing is in progress. You choose the sharing destination. RushLabs receives a copy only if you choose to send one to us. Files providers and sharing destinations may upload a copy under their own policies.

05

Deletion and retained copies

Deleting a preset can leave its earlier content in preset undo history. Delete all app records resets the calculation book and requests removal of app-owned exports and recovery copies. Cleanup pending means requested file removal is incomplete. Language/guide preferences can remain; uninstalling removes app-owned data according to iOS behavior. External originals, shared copies and device-backup copies are separate and must be managed at their destinations.

06

System services and support email

iOS backups, system diagnostics, Files providers and sharing services follow their own settings and policies. If you email RushLabs, we use the information you choose to provide to answer the request; you may request deletion of that correspondence by email. Do not include private backups, passwords or identity documents.

07

This website

These static pages have no analytics script, advertising, forms, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. Links to email open your email app. The hosting provider may process connection information under its own policies. No calculation book is submitted through this website.
