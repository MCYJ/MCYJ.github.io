Frame Pick · Privacy

Frame Pick stores an app-owned copy of the video you choose, your candidate session and generated still images locally. Your source video is not changed.

The app processes selected files on your device. It has no developer-operated collection server, advertising SDK or analytics SDK, and does not automatically send your content to RushLabs. Files stored with a cloud file provider may need to be downloaded by iOS before local processing.

When you export or share a file, you choose the destination in the iOS share sheet. The selected recipient or service may retain that copy. Review the contents and filenames before sharing.

To remove all app-local data, delete the app from your device. Exported files remain in the locations where you saved or shared them and must be removed separately. System backups may retain app data according to your settings.

If you contact RushLabs by email, we use your address and the information you send to reply. You can request deletion by writing to the same address. Do not send private photos, videos or documents.
