Get help with a photo copy

Which formats can I open?

Choose a still JPEG or PNG in Files. If the app cannot verify a file, it will not produce a copy.

What does the app remove?

It removes EXIF and XMP blocks from JPEGs and eXIf and text chunks from PNGs. When necessary it keeps a minimal orientation tag. Other metadata, and details visible in the picture itself, can remain.
