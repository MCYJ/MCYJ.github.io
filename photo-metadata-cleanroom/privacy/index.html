Cleanroom Privacy

Photos are processed on your device

Photos and copies

The app reads files you choose and prepares a separate copy on your device. RushLabs does not receive your photos or metadata. You choose where to save or share a copy.

App preferences

The app remembers whether you completed its introduction. It does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

When you share a copy with a different app or service, that recipient handles the file under its own privacy practices.
