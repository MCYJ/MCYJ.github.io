Morse Lab Privacy

Morse Lab generates audio on your device and does not use the microphone. It stores your guide preference and listening answer counts in app settings on the device.

The app does not require an account or send your practice data to RushLabs. There are no ads or analytics. You can clear listening counts from Listening Practice. Apple may back up app settings under your device settings.
