Vehicle Logbook

In development. An App Store download is not available yet.

A record behind every number

A vehicle notebook for manually entered odometer readings, fuel, charging and maintenance. Compare consumption with the distance and additions that support it.

Keep missing information visible

A partial fill is not treated as a complete interval. Missing fuel records, a changed odometer and separate energy types must remain visible instead of producing a misleading average.

Your vehicle records stay with you

The app is being developed for local storage, with no account, advertising or analytics service. Receipt handling, file exchange and reminder screens are still being completed. The pages below describe the data policy and the intended record rules; they are not a statement that every feature has shipped.
