Current availability

Vehicle Logbook is in development. There is no public download link here. Final instructions for receipt selection, CSV mapping and reminders will be added after those screens are verified.

Read consumption with its evidence

Liquid-fuel consumption uses complete fill-to-fill intervals: the distance between full fills and the relevant additions after the opening fill. The amount at the opening fill is not counted again. Partial fills remain in the interval. A missing real fill prevents a complete result; deleting a mistaken duplicate is a different action. Summaries use total additions and total distance, not the average of the individual consumption rates.

Units, energy and costs

Litres, US gallons and Imperial gallons are different units. Electricity is recorded separately from liquid fuel. Odometer replacement starts a separate segment. Costs in different currencies remain separate; the app does not fetch exchange rates. The values come from your records, not from a connected vehicle.

Backups and CSV are different

The local backup format is designed to include the record book, referenced receipt copies, drafts and undo history. Restoring replaces the current local book after validation and confirmation. A CSV is a selected set of tabular records and does not include every attachment or every piece of app state. Keep a separate backup before replacing work. A local backup is not an encrypted vault.

Reminders and manually entered distance

Reminder screens and local notification delivery are still in development. A date reminder depends on system permission and device settings. A distance threshold can only be evaluated when you enter a new odometer reading; the app does not measure your driving distance in the background.

Get help

Email your app version, iOS version and reproduction steps. For an import problem, describe the column names, number format and time zone with a small invented example. Avoid sending your real receipts, registration details, full backup or private vehicle history.
