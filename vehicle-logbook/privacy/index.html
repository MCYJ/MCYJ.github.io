Privacy

In development. An App Store download is not available yet.

Local vehicle information

Vehicle Logbook is designed to process vehicle names, manually entered odometer readings, fuel and charging additions, maintenance entries, costs, units, dates and notes on your device. RushLabs does not operate an account service, vehicle-data upload service or analytics service for this app. There is no advertising or automatic route collection.

Receipts, drafts and undo

The planned receipt feature keeps app-owned copies of files you choose. The selected external original is not modified. Local records may also include drafts, import source information, undo history, preferences and guide status. Removing a single record may leave a copy referenced by undo. These features are still being integrated and will be checked before release.

Files you choose to export

A backup can contain private vehicle history, costs and receipt copies, including information retained in undo. It is not an encrypted archive. You choose whether and where to share it. RushLabs does not receive an export unless you choose to send it to us. Files providers, sharing destinations and device backups have their own policies and may transfer a copy over the network.

Reminders

Planned reminders use local device notifications rather than a RushLabs push service. The app does not read your location or obtain automatic vehicle readings to predict a distance threshold. System permission and notification settings control delivery.

Restore and deletion

Restore is designed to validate a local backup and ask for confirmation before replacing the current book. Deletion targets app-owned records and copies; a record deletion can remain in undo until its references expire. External originals, files already shared and device-backup copies must be managed separately at their destination. If local cleanup is pending, the app must report it rather than claim that every local copy has been removed.

Support correspondence and this website

If you email RushLabs, we use the information you choose to include to answer your request. Avoid including private receipts, backups, passwords or identity documents. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email. This static website has no advertising, analytics, forms, third-party fonts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process connection information under its own policies.
