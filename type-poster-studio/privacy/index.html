Type Poster Studio privacy policy

Files on your device

The app stores your draft and a copy of each imported photo in its own storage. Your system settings may allow device backups.

Sharing your poster

A poster is generated on your device. When you share one, the app or service you choose can access the copy.

Support email

If you contact RushLabs by email, we use the message and contact details you provide to respond.
