Privacy

Cut Workbench processes cutting plans on your device.

What stays on your device

Sheet and part names, dimensions, and cutting progress are saved in the app’s local storage. Cut Workbench has no RushLabs account, analytics, or advertising.

Files you choose

The app reads a CSV when you choose it. When you share a PDF, iOS sends it to the destination you select. The destination handles that copy under its own policies.

Remove local data

Remove stock and parts using the workbench, or delete the app to remove its local data. Copies shared or saved to Files are managed separately.
